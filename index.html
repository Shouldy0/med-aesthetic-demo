FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a consultation before a treatment?

For most treatments a consultation is recommended so we can check your skin and explain what to expect. Ask when booking.

How do I book?

Call 07308 438403, email medaestheticclinicuk@gmail.com, or use the form below. Walk-ins may be available, but booking is safer.

What should I do before my appointment?

Arrive with clean skin where possible, and let us know about any allergies, medications or recent treatments beforehand.

Do you offer evening or weekend appointments?

Yes — we're open until 20:00 on weekdays and all day at the weekend.
