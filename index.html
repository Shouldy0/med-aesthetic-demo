FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know before your visit

Everything you need to know about booking, appointments, and what to expect.

Do I need a consultation before my treatment?

Yes, for most treatments we recommend a brief initial skin consultation. This ensures we understand your skin history, active ingredients you may currently use, and design a protocol best matched to your barrier health.

How do I book an appointment?

You can call us directly on 07308 438403, email us at medaestheticclinicuk@gmail.com, or submit the consultation request form below.

What should I do before arriving at my appointment?

Please arrive with clean skin where feasible. Avoid strong chemical exfoliants (retinoids, AHA/BHA peels) for 48 hours prior to facial treatments, and let our practitioners know if you have any allergies or take prescription medication.

Do you offer evening or weekend appointments?

Yes, we are open 7 days a week: Monday to Friday from 09:00 until 20:00, Saturday from 09:30 until 18:00, and Sunday from 12:00 until 17:00, making after-work and weekend bookings effortless.

Where in London is MED Aesthetic Clinic located?

We are situated on Overton Road, London SW9 7AP — approximately 10 minutes on foot from Brixton Underground Station (Victoria line) and Brixton National Rail Station.
